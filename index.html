    Quick Setup
    // From parts to "Alexa, turn on water heater" in under an hour
    01

    Buy & assemble components

    NodeMCU + SSR-25 DA + HLK-PM01 + fuse. All available on Amazon India, Robu.in, or local electronics market. Total ₹350–₹600.

    02

    Flash the firmware

    Install Arduino IDE, add ESP8266 board support, install SinricPro + NTPClient libraries. Edit config.h with your WiFi and SinricPro credentials. Upload.

    03

    Wire the AC side (or ask an electrician)

    Live wire → 25 A fuse → SSR output terminal → Water heater. Neutral passes straight through. HLK-PM01 powers the NodeMCU from mains. Follow wiring/wiring_notes.md.

    04

    Link Alexa

    In the Alexa app: Skills → search SinricPro → Enable → Discover devices. Your water heater appears as a smart switch.

    05

    Set your schedule (optional)

    Alexa app: Routines → Schedule trigger → 8:00 AM → Action: turn on water heater. The heater warms up and auto-shuts off 15 minutes later.
